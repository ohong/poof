# Design context

Poof helps people who want fewer possessions see what they own and decide what to keep, sell, donate, or toss. The creator's original README names aspiring minimalists, people doing a life reset, and people reducing consumption. Phone browsers matter.

The established interface is a warm, photographic catalog: cream paper, dark green controls, restrained terracotta accents, Instrument Serif headings, and DM Sans body text. This pass refines that existing identity. It does not establish a new brand direction. Preserve the Katalog inspiration and let the belongings carry the visual interest.

Use direct, calm language. Keep the interface quiet, readable, responsive, and precise. The requested quality bar is a well-crafted public preview, with no generic decorative chrome. Avoid tiny uppercase labels, repeated slogans, ornamental statistics, large hover zooms, and slow repeated actions.

Confirmed release scope: fully free access; private inventory; editable sale drafts; labeled suggested price ranges; copy/share; immediate, reversible Archive decisions.
