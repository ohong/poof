# Design context

Poof helps people who want fewer possessions see what they own and decide what to keep, sell, donate, or toss. The creator's original README names aspiring minimalists, people doing a life reset, and people reducing consumption. Phone browsers matter.

The November 6 Unslop Design correction replaces the incidental cream/italic-serif treatment with neutral surfaces, DM Sans, compact headings, and actual belongings. Retain the photographic catalog and green action color. Avoid decorative sparkles, forced line breaks, redundant arrows, repeated calls to action, and sentimental filler.

Use direct, calm language. Keep the interface quiet, readable, responsive, and precise. The requested quality bar is a well-crafted public preview, with no generic decorative chrome. Avoid tiny uppercase labels, repeated slogans, ornamental statistics, large hover zooms, and slow repeated actions.

Confirmed release scope: fully free access; private inventory; editable sale drafts; labeled suggested price ranges; copy/share; immediate, reversible Archive decisions. On November 6, 2026, the creator selected a public preview with the hosted backend deferred, and Resend for later sign-in email delivery. Preview changes stay in the tab. Do not imply that private accounts or uploads are live.
